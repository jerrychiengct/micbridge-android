## The story

**Your voice, amplified. Make more of the audio equipment you already have.**

MicBridge is an independent Android project by Jerry Chieng Chin Tung. It turns an Android phone and compatible speaker into a live speech and voice-effects setup. The public beta invites people to test their own equipment and help improve the app through practical feedback.

The strongest demonstration is an everyday problem: someone needs to speak to a small group and already has an Android phone and a speaker. Show how MicBridge can connect those tools, then let viewers hear the result.

## Short English description

MicBridge turns your Android phone into a live speech and voice-effects tool. Use the built-in microphone or a compatible wired, USB or wireless receiver microphone, then send your voice to a connected speaker. Speech presets, adjustable EQ, live monitoring and 16 original voice presets give you control over your sound. Download the free public beta, try your setup and help shape what comes next.

## Five reasons to feature it

1. **A practical use for existing equipment:** phone microphone plus a compatible speaker, with optional external microphone inputs.
2. **Speech first:** Lecture and Studio Speech presets, voice care and adjustable bass/mid/treble.
3. **An engaging visual demo:** live level meter, selectable audio routes and immediate control over sound.
4. **Creative variety:** 16 original presets plus pitch, grit, robot and echo controls.
5. **Community participation:** a public beta with direct developer feedback, collaboration opportunities and no brand whitelist.

## Suggested 45–60 second demonstration

| Time | What to show | Suggested narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8 s | Android phone and speaker in a small-room setup | “Can your phone and speaker help you give a short talk? This Android beta is designed to try exactly that.” |
| 8–20 s | Select mic/output, start muted, then unmute at low volume | “This is MicBridge. I’m using the phone microphone and this connected speaker.” |
| 20–35 s | Real speech with Lecture; show levels and a gentle EQ change | “There are speech presets, live monitoring and controls for gain, bass, mid and treble.” |
| 35–45 s | Switch to one creative preset, using the same spoken line | “You can also experiment with original voice effects.” |
| 45–60 s | Download page and feedback email | “It’s a free public beta. Try your equipment and tell the developer what works and what needs improving.” |

Keep the speaker away from the microphone. Start at low volume and muted. Capture the actual sound and label any separately recorded voice-over. State the phone/Android version, mic, output connection and preset used. If Bluetooth delay is noticeable, show it honestly; compare wired/USB output where available. Avoid relying on a live beta for an important event without testing your setup first.

## Accurate claims

- Current public beta: **0.5.1**, Android **8.0+**, distributed as an APK on GitHub.
- Built-in, Android-compatible wired/USB microphones and compatible wireless USB receivers; Bluetooth media, wired/USB and phone-speaker outputs.
- Audio processing stays on the phone. The app does not record microphone audio or use cloud audio processing, advertising or analytics.
- The app must stay visible during a session. It is a live audio tool, not a livestreaming, recording or background PA service.
- Compatibility and sound quality depend on the hardware and Android route. Hands-free Bluetooth mic input is excluded.
- Do not promise zero latency, universal device compatibility, AI voice restoration, an exact celebrity voice or replacement of a professional PA system.
- The repository is public. Publication of source does not itself grant an open-source licence; describe it as a public project.
- Any paid promotion or supplied benefits should be disclosed by the creator. Reviews and hardware results should reflect actual testing.
